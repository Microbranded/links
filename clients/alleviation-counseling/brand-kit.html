Tone of voice

Gentle and reassuring, never clinical or cold. Speaks to the client, not down to them. Plain language, hopeful framing, and an open invitation to begin.

Do & Don't

  • Pair Deep Teal with generous Warm Sand space
  • Use Clay / Ochre sparingly, for accents only
  • Keep ample breathing room around the mark
  • Maintain strong contrast for accessibility
  • Don't stretch, recolor, or outline the logo
  • Don't place the badge on busy photos
  • Don't mix in unrelated bright/neon colors
